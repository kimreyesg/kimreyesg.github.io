    <p class="eyebrow">Greis J. Kim Reyes, Ph.D.</p>
    <h2>Assistant Professor of Physics</h2>
    <p class="about-affiliation">Department of Physics and Astronomy · SUNY New Paltz</p>

    <p>
      I am a computational materials physicist interested in understanding how electronic structure, defects, and bonding determine the properties of semiconductor and quantum materials. My research uses first-principles calculations and related computational methods to connect atomic-scale physics with measurable electronic, optical, and magnetic behavior.
    </p>

    <p>
      Teaching is an important part of my work. I aim to combine physical intuition, problem solving, and computation so students can move between equations, simulations, and the behavior of real materials.
    </p>
